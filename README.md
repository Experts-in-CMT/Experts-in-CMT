# Experts in CMT

**Clear Answers for a Confusing Disease.**

[**Visit the site: expertsincmt.org**](https://expertsincmt.org) · [Changelog](themes/expertsincmt/CHANGELOG.md) · License: All rights reserved

Experts in CMT is an educational platform for Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease (CMT), a rare, inheritable peripheral neuropathy that affects about 1 in 2,500 people. CMT comprises more than 170 distinct, clinically described subtypes caused by over 140 genes, making it hard to understand and easy to get lost in. The platform brings both the science and the everyday reality of the disease into one place, in plain language, so you do not have to know the right words to find the right information.

## Overview

CMT is genetically diverse and clinically complex, and most of what is known about it lives in technical databases and specialist journals. Experts in CMT curates that material from primary sources and pairs it with clear educational writing, so a newly diagnosed patient, a family member, a clinician, and a researcher can each find what they need without translating medical jargon first.

## Intent-aware search

A custom search engine is built into the site to understand what you mean, not just match the words you type. Ask for a subtype, a gene, a symptom, or an inheritance pattern in whatever words you have, and it resolves your intent and takes you to the right answer. It is the engine behind the promise that you should not need the exact terminology to find the information.

Results appear live as you type, matched terms are highlighted in context, and a near-miss is corrected to the closest subtype or gene rather than turned away. Broad searches hand off into the Subtype Browser or Gene Browser with the matching filter already applied, and even a search that matches nothing offers guided starting points instead of a dead end.

## Explore the genetics

- **CMT Subtype Browser:** a searchable, filterable catalog of every named CMT subtype. Filter by CMT type, gene, inheritance pattern, neuropathy type, or chromosome, and open any subtype for its full profile.
- **Gene Browser:** one entry per CMT gene, with its full name, cytogenetic location, the subtypes it causes, and links to the authoritative external records.
- **Variant Mechanisms:** for each subtype, how its variants are understood to cause disease (loss of function, dominant-negative, toxic gain of function, or complex), with the reasoning and confidence level behind each.
- **Genetic Testing Guide:** what genetic testing for CMT involves, and the popular tests providers use.

## Understand the disease

- **The Dorsal Root:** long-form articles on the science of CMT and the people behind it, across awareness, genetics, research, and news.
- **Learn:** plain-language explainers such as *What is CMT?* and *CMT and Breathing*, answering the questions people actually ask.
- **Glossary:** clear definitions of the terms that show up across the site and in the clinic.

## Data and sources

The genetic records are curated by hand from the primary genetic and clinical literature and then reconciled against major reference databases, including HGNC, OMIM, ClinGen, ClinVar, GeneReviews, Orphanet, PanelApp, UniProt, and gnomAD. Entries are updated as the science changes and are published as structured, citable records so they can be referenced and attributed with confidence.
